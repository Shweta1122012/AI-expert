About SIGNAI & Architectural Overview

ASL vs ISL: American Sign Language (ASL) and Indian Sign Language (ISL) are distinct natural languages with unique grammatical structures, finger-spelling conventions, and vocabularies. While ASL fingerspelling primarily uses a one-handed alphabet system, ISL heavily incorporates two-handed manual sign structures for letters and numbers.

Local Privacy Commitment

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy: All video frames, hand landmark calculations, and gesture classifications occur entirely within your local browser memory using WebGL and MediaPipe WebAssembly modules. Zero video frames are ever transmitted to any external server.

Assistive Technology Disclaimer

This system is an experimental assistive computer-vision technology prototype and should not be relied upon as a substitute for certified human sign language interpretation in medical, legal, or emergency situations.
